Scenario 1: Greeting & Escorting Patient

Med Student
不好意思让你久等了，请跟我来。
Bù hǎo yìsi ràng nín jiǔ děng le, qǐng gēn wǒ lái.
Sorry to keep you waiting, please follow me.
Patient
没事，我只等了三十分钟。
Méishì, wǒ zhǐ děngle sānshí fēnzhōng.
No worries, I only waited 30 minutes.

Scenario 2: Calling an Interpreter

Med Student
你好，我是医学生。我只会说一点点中文，让我找一名翻译。
Nǐ hǎo, wǒ shì yī xuéshēng. Wǒ zhǐ huì shuō yī diǎn diǎn Zhōngwén, ràng wǒ zhǎo yī míng fān yì.
Hello, I am a medical student. I only speak a little Chinese, let me find an interpreter.
Nurse
别担心，我是一名护士，我会说中文。
Bié dān xīn, wǒ shì yì míng hùshì, wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén.
Don't worry, I am a nurse and I can speak Chinese.

Scenario 3: 60yo Female Headache (CC & HPI)

Doctor
我是医学生。你为什么来看医生？能指一指哪里痛吗？
Wǒ shì yī xuéshēng. Nǐ wèi shén me lái kàn yī shēng? Néng zhǐ yī zhǐ nǎ lǐ tòng ma?
I am a medical student. Why are you coming to see the doctor? Could you point to where it hurts?
Patient
头痛。什么时候开始？五天前。从一到十，我有八分痛。
Tóu tòng. Sān ge xiǎo shí qián kāi shǐ... Cóng yī dào shí, wǒ yǒu bā fēn tòng.
Headache for 5 days. On a scale of 1 to 10, I am at an 8.

Scenario 4: 45yo Male Back Pain (Breakout Practice)

Doctor
你好。我是医学生。你叫什么？你为什么来看医生？
Nǐ hǎo. Wǒ shì yī xuéshēng. Nǐ jiào shén me? Nǐ wèi shén me lái kàn yī shēng?
Patient
你好。我的背痛。三个小时前开始。
Nǐ hǎo. Wǒ de bèi tòng. Sān ge xiǎo shí qián kāi shǐ.
Hello. My back hurts. It started 3 hours ago.
Doctor
从一到十，你有多痛？什么会加重你的痛？什么会减少你的痛？
Cóng yī dào shí, nǐ yǒu duō tòng? Shén me huì jiā zhòng nǐ de tòng? Shén me huì jiǎn shǎo nǐ de tòng?

Scenario 5: 65yo Female Comprehensive PMH, FH & SH Workup

Doctor
你以前有健康问题吗？你有高血压或糖尿病吗？
Nǐ yǐ qián yǒu jiàn kāng wèn tí ma? Nǐ yǒu gāo xuě yā huò táng niào bìng ma?
Doctor
你的家人有健康问题吗？你做工吗？你抽烟还是喝酒吗？
Nǐ de jiā rén yǒu jiàn kāng wèn tí ma? Nǐ zuò gōng ma? Nǐ chōu yān hái shì hē jiǔ ma?
