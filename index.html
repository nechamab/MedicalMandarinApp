Doctor

Nǐ hǎo. Wǒ shì yī xuéshēng. Nǐ jiào shén me? Nǐ wèi shén me lái kàn yī shēng?
Patient
我的背痛。三个小时前开始。
Wǒ de bèi tòng. Sān ge xiǎo shí qián kāi shǐ. (My back hurts. Started 3 hours ago.)
Doctor
从一到十，你有多痛？什么会加重你的痛？
Cóng yī dào shí, nǐ yǒu duō tòng? Shén me huì jiā zhòng nǐ de tòng?
Mandarin Sentence Structure Rules

1. Basic Timeline Order:
Subject + Time (When) + Location + Verb + Object

我早上九点会去看医生。
Wǒ zǎoshang jiǔ diǎn huì qù kàn yīshēng. (I will see doctor at 9am.)

2. Time Duration Rule:
Subject + Verb + Time Duration

我讲了三个小时。
Wǒ jiǎngle sān gè xiǎoshí. (I talked for 3 hours.)

3. Giving Directions:
Subject + 在 (zài) + Direction

医院在左边。
Yīyuàn zài zuǒbiān. (The hospital is on the left.)
